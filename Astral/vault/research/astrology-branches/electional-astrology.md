# Electional Astrology — Choosing the Right Moment

## Core Principle
Electional astrology = choosing the best astrological timing for an action, event, or ceremony.

Unlike natal astrology (who are you?) or horary (what will happen?), electional asks: "When should I?"

## Historical Context
- Ancient practice: Babylonian, Hellenistic, Vedic (Muhurta)
- Medieval Europe: Albubather, Ibn Ezra texts on "electing times"
- Hindu tradition: Jyotish Muhurta for marriages, house entries, business openings

## Key Rules

### Basic Do's
- Ascendant strong: rising sign dignified, ruling planet angular
- Moon favorable: waxing, not void-of-course, no hard aspects to malefics
- Benefics angular: Jupiter/Venus in angles = protection
- Lord of the 1st and lord of the 7th in agreement = harmony

### Red Flags
- Saturn on Ascendant = obstacles/delay
- Mars on Ascendant = accidents, conflict
- Moon void-of-course = nothing comes of it
- Malefic on Midheaven = bad reputation for enterprise
- 8th lord prominent = loss, danger

### Special Considerations by Event
| Event | Priority |
|---|---|
| Marriage | Venus, 7th house, Moon, benefics |
| Business launch | 10th house, Jupiter, Mercury |
| Surgery | Mars/Saturn avoidance, Moon strong |
| Travel | Moon strong, 9th house, Mercury |
| Legal | Saturn/Jupiter balance, 9th/10th |

## House Significance for Timing
- 1st: personal action
- 7th: partnerships/contracts
- 10th: public enterprise/reputation
- 4th: real estate/family matters
- 9th: long journeys/law/study

## Vedic Muhurta Additions
- Abhijit muhurta (96 mins around noon) = always auspicious
- Avoid Choghadiya inauspicious periods
- Tithi (lunar day) quality
- Nakshatra quality at start time

## Tools
- Ephemeris + tables of houses
- Software: Janus, Solar Fire, Astro.com Electional tool
- Moon phase: favor waxing over waning

## Relation to Other Branches
- Builds on natal: use YOUR chart, not just general electional
- Connects mundane: eclipse avoidance
- Overlaps horary: horary answers timing questions directly

## Q&A Format (Common Questions)

Q: Should I launch during retrograde?
A: Business launches during Mercury retro = rework/redesign likely. Prefer direct motion. Exception: inner review/overhaul projects.

Q: New Moon vs Full Moon for starting?
A: New Moon = new beginnings (most common). Full Moon = completion/release.

Q: Can any time be made auspicious?
A: Skilled electors can mitigate hard charts, but some conditions (e.g. eclipse, Saturn rising) are extremely challenging.
