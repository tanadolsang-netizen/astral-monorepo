# Financial / Business Astrology — Cosmos & Markets

## Core Principle
Financial astrology applies astrological cycles to markets, stocks, commodities, and business timing. Two main approaches: traditional Western and Jyotish-based.

## Key Cycles

### Jupiter-Saturn Conjunction (20-year cycle)
- Marks major economic eras
- Recent conjunctions: 1980 (Libra), 2000 (Taurus), 2020 (Aquarius)
- 2020 conjunction in Aquarius = tech/digital era acceleration

### Sunspot Cycle (~11 years)
- Correlates with market mood
- Solar maximum = optimism; solar minimum = caution

### Venus Cycle (~584 days)
- Morning Star / Evening Star phases
- Venus direct = buying favorable
- Venus retrograde = reassessment/revision

## Planetary Significations in Markets

| Planet | Market Effect |
|---|---|
| Jupiter | Expansion, bull market, growth sectors |
| Saturn | Contraction, bear market, restriction |
| Mercury | Volatility, trading volume, communications |
| Mars | Sudden moves, conflict-driven markets |
| Venus | Luxury, art, consumer confidence |
| Uranus | Technology disruption, surprise earnings |
| Neptune | Illusion, bubbles, pharmaceuticals |
| Pluto | Systemic change, power restructuring |

## Common Techniques

### 1. Ingress Charts
- Jupiter into sign = sector rotation
- Saturn into sign = contraction in that sector

### 2. Eclipse Effects
- Eclipses in cardinal signs = major market shifts
- Path of eclipse = affected regions

### 3. Lunations
- New Moon = trend beginning
- Full Moon = trend culmination
- Eclipse = extreme turning point

### 4. Retrograde Periods
- Mercury retrograde = volatility, delays
- Venus retrograde = revaluation of value
- Mars retrograde = market reassessment

## Vedic / Jyotish Financial Astrology
- Muhurta for business openings
- Dasha periods for individual investor timing
- Saturn/Mars periods = caution; Jupiter/Venus = opportunity
- Panchang (daily almanac) for trading timing

## Famous Practitioners
- W.D. Gann: geometry + astrology in markets
- R.N. Elliott: wave theory influenced by Fibonacci + cycles
- Louise McWhirter: Moon's nodes in markets
- Bradley Feed: stock market timing service (historical)

## Limitations
- Astrology = probability, not certainty
- Free will + mass psychology override individual charts
- Best used with technical/fundamental analysis, not alone

## Relation to Other Branches
- Uses mundane astrology for global trends
- Electional for timing trades/business launches
- Vedic dasha for individual timing
