# Astrocartography — Planetary Geography

## Core Principle
Astrocartography = map your natal chart onto the world. Each planet has "lines" crossing the Earth's surface at specific latitudes/longitudes where that planet's energy is most active for you.

## Inventor
Jim Lewis (1941-1995) developed modern astrocartography in 1970s-80s. Built on earlier ideas from Bradley and Weiss.

## How Lines Work
When a planet in your natal chart is on an angle (Ascendant, Descendant, MC, IC) at a specific location, that location activates that planet strongly.

### Four Line Types
| Line | Meaning | Experience |
|---|---|---|
| Ascendant line | Planet rising | You embody that planet |
| Descendant line | Planet setting | You attract that energy from others |
| MC line | Planet at midheaven | Planet drives your public life |
| IC line | Planet at nadir | Planet in private/home life |

## Practical Application

### Choosing a Place to Live
- Saturn line = disciplined, structured, but isolated
- Jupiter line = expansive, opportunity, growth
- Venus line = charming, artistic, relationship-focused
- Mars line = active, competitive, fast-paced

### Choosing a Place to Visit
- 1-2 weeks on benefic line (Jupiter/Venus) = vacation boost
- Avoid Saturn lines for leisure (depression, isolation)
