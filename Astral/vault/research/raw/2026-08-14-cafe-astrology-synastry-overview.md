## What the article covers

A general-technique overview of synastry (relationship astrology): what it is, how aspect types are read between two charts, and which planet pairings matter most.

## What synastry is

"Synastry is the art of relationship Astrology" — comparing two natal charts against each other rather than reading either chart alone. Explicitly framed as going well beyond sun-sign compatibility.

## The four aspect types and how they read in synastry

- **Conjunctions** — powerful points of merging; partners often feel immediate recognition/similarity where their planets conjoin.
- **Oppositions** — polarity and attraction; can pull partners into a "see-saw" dynamic of competition or complementary counterbalancing.
- **Sextiles and trines** ("flowing" aspects) — ease in blending the two planetary energies; pleasant, low-friction, though not always the source of initial spark.
- **Squares** — friction requiring active integration work; frustrating but can fuel excitement and growth for a couple oriented toward development rather than comfort.

## Which planet pairings matter most

- **Venus-Sun** — harmony and contentment.
- **Venus-Mars** — the classic sexual-magnetism pairing; flowing aspects (sextile/trine) = natural attraction, hard aspects (square/opposition) = magnetism with friction.
- **Venus-Moon** — smooths friction through familiarity/companionship, though on hard aspects this can also mean unresolved issues get glossed over rather than addressed.
- **Jupiter** contacts — broadly beneficial across most pairings, fosters goodwill and forgiveness.
- **Saturn** contacts — the relationship's "glue" (commitment, endurance, seriousness), but requires care — Venus-Saturn specifically can feel restrictive even as it binds.
- Outer planets (Neptune, Pluto, Uranus) — described as generating dynamic, evolving, less stable interactions.
