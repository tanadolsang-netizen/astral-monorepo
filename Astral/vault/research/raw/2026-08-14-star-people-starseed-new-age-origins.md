## What the article covers

The origin and belief structure of the "star people" / "starseed" concept that later New Age writers folded the "144,000 lightworkers" idea into.

## Origins

- Term introduced by **Brad Steiger** in his 1976 book *Gods of Aquarius*, describing "humans who come from a special gene pool linked to visits by extraterrestrials."
- **Sheldan Nidle** (founder of the Ground Crew Project) is another cited popularizer/advocate.
- Broader web search (not from this article, but corroborating) traces "lightworker" specifically to spiritual mentor **Michael Mirdad** in the early 1980s, later popularized by **Doreen Virtue**; "starseed" typology was further expanded by **Dolores Cannon** and **Barbara Marciniak** through hypnotic-regression-based channeling work in the 1980s–90s.

## Core beliefs

- Star people/starseeds are framed as alien-human hybrids or souls with extraterrestrial origin, who incarnated on Earth either by ordinary birth or as a "walk-in" to an existing adult body.
- Adherents typically describe **amnesia** about their true origin followed by an **"awakening process"** that restores memory/purpose — structurally identical to the "ตื่นรู้" (awakening) framing used for lightworkers/starseeds in the vault's own note.
- The belief carries an implicit **spiritual mission** framing: star people are on Earth for a purpose tied to planetary/consciousness evolution, not by accident.
- Journalist Joel Achenbach is quoted characterizing starseed belief as psychologically "looking inward," contrasting with classic ufology's outward-looking search for external proof — i.e., starseed belief functions more as an identity/meaning framework than an empirical claim.

## Relationship to "144,000" and "lightworker"

This specific article doesn't mention the 144,000 figure directly (it predates that particular numeric framing, which appears to be a later syncretic layer blending Revelation's number onto the starseed/lightworker concept). The "lightworker" term is a sibling/overlapping concept: broader web research indicates "all starseeds are lightworkers, but not all lightworkers are starseeds" — lightworker is the general vocation (consciousness-raising service), starseed specifies an extraterrestrial-soul-origin belief.
