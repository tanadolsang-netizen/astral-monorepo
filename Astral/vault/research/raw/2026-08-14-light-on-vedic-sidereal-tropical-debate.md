## What the article covers

A head-to-head explainer of why tropical (Western) and sidereal (Vedic) astrology assign planets to different signs for the same birth moment, the astronomical mechanism behind the gap, and an argued conclusion about which is "correct."

## Tropical system's case

- Anchored to the Sun/Earth relationship and the seasons: 0° Aries = the Vernal Equinox (~March 21), full stop, regardless of where the actual constellation Aries currently sits in the sky.
- Explicitly "symbolic" rather than astronomically literal — the zodiac is a seasonal/psychological coordinate system, not a star map.
- Argued to be better suited to describing personality and psychological patterns; most Western reference texts are built around psychological interpretation.

## Sidereal system's case

- Anchored to the actual, observable fixed-star constellations — where the planets visually are, astronomically.
- The article credits ancient Vedic astronomer-sages with having calculated Earth's axial wobble (precession) "centuries before modern technology" existed to measure it directly — presented as evidence sidereal astrology's astronomical grounding predates telescopic instrumentation.
- Argued to be more accurate for **predicting actual events** (as opposed to psychological description), and Vedic reference texts are built around prediction rather than personality.
- Vedic practice weights the **Moon and Ascendant** more heavily than the Sun — a structurally different emphasis from tropical Western astrology's Sun-centric popular culture.

## The mechanism: precession of the equinoxes (ayanamsha)

Earth's rotational axis wobbles slowly (~50.3 arcseconds/year), causing the equinox point to drift backward through the background stars over time. This drift is the **ayanamsha** — currently roughly **23–24 degrees** between the tropical and sidereal zero points. The two systems were last aligned around **285 CE**; since then tropical Aries (season-anchored) has drifted away from sidereal Aries (star-anchored) by that growing margin. This is the same ayanamsha concept the user's own `scripts/transit_now.py` applies (Lahiri ayanamsha) to compute Thai sidereal positions alongside tropical ones.

## Author's conclusion (not neutral, but not dogmatic either)

The author does not declare one system objectively correct. Stated position: **"the accuracy of any system depends more on the clarity of intuition of the astrologer than on the system itself."** Argues the two systems don't actually contradict when a full chart is read holistically — each is treated as a different but internally consistent symbolic map onto the same underlying archetypal pattern, rather than competing claims about physical reality.
