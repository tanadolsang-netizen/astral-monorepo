# Chinese Astrology & BaZi (八字)

## Core Principle
Chinese astrology = 12 animal signs + 5 elements + Yin/Yang cycling through time. BaZi (八字) = "Eight Characters" from birth year/month/day/hour.

## Four Pillars (BaZi)
Each pillar = 2 characters (heavenly stem + earthly branch):

| Pillar | Represents |
|---|---|
| Year Pillar | Ancestors, society, outer world |
| Month Pillar | Parents, early life, work environment |
| Day Pillar | Self, spouse (Day Master = you) |
| Hour Pillar | Children, old age, inner world |

## 10 Heavenly Stems
- Yang: Jia (甲), Yi (乙), Bing (丙), Ding (丁), Wu (戊)
- Yin: Ji (己), Geng (庚), Xin (辛), Ren (壬), Gui (癸)
- Each has element + yin/yang

## 12 Earthly Branches
- Zi (鼠/Rat), Chou (牛/Ox), Yin (虎/Tiger), Mao (兔/Rabbit)
- Chen (龙/Dragon), Si (蛇/Snake), Wu (马/Horse), Wei (羊/Goat)
- Shen (猴/Monkey), You (鸡/Rooster), Xu (狗/Dog), Hai (猪/Pig)

## 5 Elements (Wu Xing)
| Element | Planet | Direction | Season | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wood | Jupiter | East | Spring | Green |
| Fire | Mars | South | Summer | Red |
| Earth | Saturn | Center | Transition | Yellow |
| Metal | Venus | West | Autumn | White |
| Water | Mercury | North | Winter | Black/Blue |

## Yin-Yang Cycle
- Each sign + stem is either Yin or Yang
- Yang: odd numbers, masculine, active
- Yin: even numbers, feminine, receptive
- Balance between Yin/Yang in chart = harmony

## Key Combinations

### Favourable
- Day Master strong with resource elements
- Output (EG) and wealth (FC) both present
- Support (Rob/SE) from chart

### Unfavourable
- Day Master weak with no support
- Clashing elements dominate
- Punishment combinations

## BaZi vs Western Astrology
| Feature | BaZi | Western |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | Time of birth (4 pillars) | Time/place of birth |
| Elements | 5 (Wood/Fire/Earth/Metal/Water) | 4 (Fire/Earth/Air/Water) |
| Planets | 10 stems + 12 branches | 10 planets + 12 signs |
| Timing | 10-year luck pillars | Transits, progressions |
| Focus | Destiny + timing | Personality + cycles |

## Use Cases
- Career direction (output elements)
- Relationship compatibility (Day Master interaction)
- Health (element imbalance)
- Timing of major life events (luck pillars)
