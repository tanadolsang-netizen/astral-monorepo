## What the article covers

The **house overlay** technique in synastry — a distinct method from aspect comparison, explaining what it means when one person's planet falls inside a specific house of the other person's natal chart.

## What a house overlay is

Where aspect comparison (Venus-Mars sextile, Sun square Sun, etc.) looks at angular relationships between two people's planets, house overlay asks: *which of the other person's life areas (houses) does my planet "activate"?* It's directional and asymmetric — Person A's Venus in Person B's 7th house is a different statement than Person B's Venus in Person A's 7th house.

## Venus in partner's 7th house

- The Venus person sees the house person as "an ideal type of partner" — someone embodying the traits they're drawn to in a significant other.
- Framed as complementary: "where one of you is weak, the other is strong."
- Explicitly called "a powerful overlay for marriage" — one of the strongest romantic-compatibility house placements in the overlay system, especially when mutual (both people's Venus lands in the other's 7th).

## Brief coverage of 8th and 9th house Venus overlays

- **8th house:** intense sexual attraction and emotional transformation through the relationship (the 8th house governs intimacy, merging, transformation).
- **9th house:** shared belief/worldview compatibility and a friendly, expansive dynamic — "the house person probably sees you as someone with whom a future is possible."
