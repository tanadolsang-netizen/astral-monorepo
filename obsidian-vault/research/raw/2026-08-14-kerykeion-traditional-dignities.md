## What the article covers

A dense, technical explainer of the **essential dignities** system — the traditional (pre-modern, Hellenistic/medieval) method for scoring how well a planet functions given the zodiac sign it occupies at birth — plus the related concept of **sect**.

## Essential dignities (five-level hierarchy)

1. **Domicile** — a planet in the sign it rules. Described as being "at home, with full access to its own tools" — maximum strength/ease of expression.
2. **Exaltation** — a planet in a sign where its function is "honored and amplified," a step below domicile but still strongly favorable.
3. **Triplicity** — dignity by elemental grouping (fire/earth/air/water signs), assigned differently depending on whether the chart is a day or night chart (ties into sect, below).
4. **Terms/Bounds** — each sign is divided into unequal degree-ranges, each assigned to a different planet; a planet in its own bound gets a smaller dignity boost.
5. **Face/Decan** — each sign split into three 10° segments, each ruled by a different planet; weakest of the five dignity levels.

The flip side — **debility** (detriment: opposite the ruled sign; fall: opposite the exaltation sign) — is reframed constructively: a planet in detriment/fall isn't simply "bad," it tends to force "more creative, unconventional, or hard-won forms of expression" because it can't rely on default, comfortable expression.

## Sect

Charts split into **diurnal (day)** or **nocturnal (night)** categories based on whether the Sun is above or below the horizon at birth. Planets aligned with their chart's sect (e.g., a diurnal planet like Jupiter in a day chart) tend to "express their themes more smoothly." Misalignment doesn't disable a planet, but adds friction that requires more conscious integration to work with well.

## Interpretive point worth keeping

The article makes an explicit value judgment that's more sophisticated than typical dignity write-ups: **"automatic expression" from a well-dignified planet can lack depth**, while a poorly-dignified or off-sect planet, precisely because it can't coast, can produce more "genuine depth and resilience" when the person consciously works with it. This reframes dignity/debility as a difficulty-dial rather than a good/bad grade.
